Skill practice

Rate fit, readiness, and oversight as separate marks

A single AI score averages away the reason a decision is stuck. Rate each mark on its own, so each one points to a different action.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. Five recurring decisions are on your map, each with the loss it carries. Rate how well AI fits each one, how ready the company is today, and how much review a wrong call needs. Value is already placed. Do not read fit from it.

Form of AI
What AI does to the decision. Predict estimates an uncertain outcome. Sense flags a condition. Optimize and recommend compares feasible options against a stated objective. Generate produces content that informs a choice. Act within bounds carries out an authorized choice.
Fit
How far some form could change the choice at the moment it is made. It needs repeated occasions, an uncertainty or set of options the form can address, an objective that can be stated, and a route from output to action. Outside current reach means no form can change the choice yet because one of those is missing.
Readiness
The present state of data, process and decision rights, people, and route to action. A blocker is the one missing prerequisite.
Oversight need
The review a wrong call requires, given its consequence, how reversible it is, the time to review, and who bears the error.
