Set the automation level

Every exception response can sit at a defensible level of automation, and keeping the decision human while automating only the alert is one of those levels. The move that comes first is to say, response by response, what holds today, what could hold once named preconditions are met, and which condition stops it going higher.

Levels and the conditions that cap them

Sense
The system raises an alert that something changed. People do everything else.
Interpret
The system also says what the change means for a specific order or component, and prepares the case. A person chooses and acts.
Recommend
The system also proposes a response and estimates its cost. A person decides and carries it out.
Bounded action
The system carries out a response that is reversible, preauthorized, and limited. Someone set the limits in advance.
Broad autonomous action
The system chooses and carries out responses without those limits.

Four conditions cap the level

Stable, testable rules
The decision reduces to rules that can be written down and tested.
Reversible consequences
A wrong action can be undone.
Delegated authority
Someone with the authority has delegated the action in fact. A credential that permits an action is not a delegation.
Verifiable outcomes
Someone can check the results at the volume involved.

An agent is software that selects and carries out workflow steps without a person choosing each. The levels apply to an agent as much as to any other software.
