Test the review

A review is oversight only when the record shows that the reviewer could inspect the evidence, could change the outcome, had time to check, and caught errors. The move that comes first is to read a claim of review against the record, test by test, before anyone builds on it.

Terms used here

Acknowledgement
A click that marks an alert as seen.
Seeded error
A wrong alert planted on purpose, so that someone can check whether reviewers catch it.
Action divergence
How often the action taken differs from what the system proposed.
Trace
The record of what a reviewer checked and what followed from it.
