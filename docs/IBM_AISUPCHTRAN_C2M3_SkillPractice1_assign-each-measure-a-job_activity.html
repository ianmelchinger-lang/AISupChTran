Skill practice · Error measure choice

Assign each measure a job

Every forecast error measure decides which misses count most. Before a measure judges a forecasting change, you need to know what it hides at the level where the forecast is used, and give it a job to match.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. The steering group wants a measure set for judging any change to the baseline forecast across all four product lines. Planners set that forecast for each product at each location, month by month. Five candidate measures are on the table.

Primary
The one measure that judges the change.
Reporting only, with cuts
Kept for context, always shown beside the line or segment results it could otherwise hide.
Reject for this decision
Not fit to judge a forecast used for each product at each location.
Naive forecast
Demand in the same period last year, used as a fixed benchmark.

Five candidate measures

For each measure, choose what it hides in this portfolio, then the job it should have. Check when you are ready; the check runs again as you change answers.
