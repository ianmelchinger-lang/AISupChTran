Skill practice

Bound the loss a better forecast could remove

A value estimate multiplies three factors. When one factor describes different errors from the others, the estimate inflates, the saving falls short, and finance discounts every estimate that follows.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. Deployment is the daily and weekly choice of how much of each SKU to send to each distribution center. Sometimes stock sits in the wrong place and a premium shipment fixes it. A proposal would predict demand at each location, so deployment starts from a better forecast. Bound what that proposal could remove.

Value at stake
The loss a decision's errors cause over a stated period that a better choice could have avoided. You estimate it as occasions × cost of error per occasion × improvable share.
Occasion
One choice that ended in the error. Count choices, not rows of data.
Improvable share
The part of that loss a given form of AI could remove. Here the form is prediction.
