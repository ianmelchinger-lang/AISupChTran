A forecasting proposal is judged soundly when the problem is stated in operational terms, the moves run in an order their inputs allow, and each sign of success can be checked on the finished evaluation record. Sort the cards, then sequence the moves.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. Three proposals claim to improve the baseline forecast, the monthly forecast for each item at each location: an internal pilot, Vendor A’s platform, and Vendor B’s demand sensing. Each sponsor sent an accuracy figure measured its own way. The senior vice president wants a recommendation for each. Fifteen cards follow. Some belong on the path to an evaluation you can defend, and some are tempting moves the path rules out.
