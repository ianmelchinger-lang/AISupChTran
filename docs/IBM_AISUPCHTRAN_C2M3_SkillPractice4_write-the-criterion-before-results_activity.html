Skill practice · Pre-set decision criterion

Write the criterion before results

Every term left open until the readout is a choice someone can make after seeing the result, and any one of them can turn a flat result into a pass. A criterion fixes those terms in advance and includes a rule that can return stop.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. The forecasting pilot on the Home Care line is held at “not yet.” Before anyone looks at its restated results, you are drafting the criterion that will judge any change to the Home Care baseline forecast. Planners use that forecast for each product at each location, month by month.

Results withheld. You will see how your criterion judges the pilot only after you lock it.

Forecast of record

Guardrail
A measure that may not get worse beyond a stated margin while the primary measure improves.
Forecast value added
Each stage of the forecast, from the naive benchmark to the final number, scored on the same measure, to show which stages help.
Non-compensatory rule
No measure’s gain can offset another’s breach.
