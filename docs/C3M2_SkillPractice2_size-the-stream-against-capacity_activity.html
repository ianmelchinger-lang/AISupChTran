Size the stream against capacity

An alert stream is worth what its actionable share delivers against the time people have to investigate it. The move that comes first is arithmetic: how many alerts the minutes can reach, and whether the shortfall is minutes or the stream itself.

Terms used here

Actionable alert
An alert that leads someone to act on a real exception.
Precision
The share of alerts that are actionable.
Fit
Whether an alert reaches the role that owns the decision it calls for.
Capacity
The investigations a day that the available minutes allow.
Coverage
Capacity as a share of the whole stream.
Interrupt, queue, digest, suppress
What happens to an alert type. An interrupted or queued alert is investigated by a person. A digest is one daily summary that a person scans, so it uses none of the investigation minutes here. A suppressed type is sent to no one.
Retuning
Raising a threshold or merging repeats so that a type fires less often.
