Skill practice

Test what each claim supplies before it moves the map

A map earns trust when each placement can be challenged on a named factor. A statement counts as evidence only when it supplies a factor and a source. Conviction, speed, and a total with no baseline are preference, and the card should say so.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. Six items reach you from the steering group's view of the map. For each, decide which factor it supplies, what the evidence card is missing first, and what you would do with the placement.

Evidence card
The factors behind a placement, with unit, period, source, and range, plus who owns each assumption and the observation that would move it.
Factors
Frequency (how many occasions), cost of error (what one wrong call costs), improvable share (the part a form could remove), readiness, and oversight need.
No map factor
A true statement that enters none of the factors, such as how fast a task runs.
Preference only
Conviction, or a total with no baseline behind it.
Measured baseline
A count or amount of the loss as it stands today.
