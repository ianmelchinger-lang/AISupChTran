Skill practice · Forecast error to safety stock

Bound the buffer benefit

A better forecast does not lower inventory by itself. Safety stock is sized from how widely forecast misses vary over the replenishment lead time. A narrower spread can shrink that buffer or raise service, not both in full, and only where the buffer is actually reset.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. The demand planning manager has drafted a benefit statement for the Home Care forecasting pilot. It assumes that the spread of forecast misses over the replenishment lead time narrows by one-tenth. That is the draft’s assumption, not a result. Decide how strongly each claim may be written.
