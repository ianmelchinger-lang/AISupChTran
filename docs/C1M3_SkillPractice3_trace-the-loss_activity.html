Skill practice

Record each loss once, where a different choice would have prevented it

A loss appears where money is booked, often far from the choice that caused it. When every linked decision claims the loss, a function's AI case adds up to more than the company lost.

The situation

You are the supply chain transformation lead at Sample Enterprise Goods. Four consequences sit on your map. For each, mark the decision where a different choice would have prevented it, the decisions the error passed through, and the decision where it was booked. Then check the ledger against what the books recorded.

Origin
The decision where a controllable error starts. It is the only decision that claims the loss.
Transmitter
A decision the error passes through on its way to being booked.
Booking point
The decision where the money shows up in the books.
Ledger
The total credited to origins, compared with what was booked.
